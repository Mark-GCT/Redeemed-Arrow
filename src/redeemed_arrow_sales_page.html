Questions

Frequently asked

Is this therapy or clinical services?
No. Redeemed Arrow is a consulting and education community — not a clinical mental health service and not a substitute for therapy. We provide behavior strategies, parent training, and support rooted in ABA and trauma-informed frameworks. If your child needs clinical services, we encourage you to pursue those alongside membership here.
What if I'm not a licensed foster parent yet?
You are still welcome here. Many of our members are in the process of getting licensed or are considering fostering. The training vault and community are valuable at any stage of the journey.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my tier later?
Yes. You can change your membership tier at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.
What does the free week include?
Your free week gives you full access to the tier you select — including the training vault, community, and resources. No credit card is required to start. At the end of 7 days you can continue by entering your payment details or cancel with no charge.
Is the community faith-based?
Redeemed Arrow is faith-informed — rooted in a sense of calling and purpose — but the community is open and welcoming to all foster families regardless of faith background. The tools, strategies, and support are built for every foster parent.
Does my agency qualify for a group license?
Yes. We offer group licensing for foster care agencies who want to provide this as a resource for their families. Contact us directly to discuss pricing and setup for your organization.
