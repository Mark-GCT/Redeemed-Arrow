Who, What & Why

Is Community Membership Right for Your Family?

Community membership is the foundation every Redeemed Arrow plan is built on. Here's exactly who it's for, what it includes, and why members lean on it between coaching sessions.

  • Who It's For

    Every Redeemed Arrow member, on every plan. Whether you just joined Supported or you're on the Renewed plan with private coaching, community access is already part of your membership.

    A private space on Circle where you can post questions, share wins, and hear from other foster, adoptive, and kinship parents and our team, any day, any time, right from our home page.

Common Questions

Community Membership FAQ

What is community membership?

Community membership is ongoing access to Redeemed Arrow's private online community, where foster, adoptive, and kinship parents connect, ask questions, and support each other outside of scheduled coaching calls.

Is community membership included in every plan?

Yes. Community membership is included in all three plans, Supported, Equipped, and Renewed, so every member has it from day one with no upgrade required.

What platform does the Redeemed Arrow community use?

The Redeemed Arrow community lives on Circle, a private community platform used to host our members' discussions and support threads.

How do I access the community?

Once you're a member, you can open the community anytime using the Circle community launcher on our home page.

How is community membership different from group or individual coaching?

Community membership is an always-on discussion space included with every plan. Group coaching, included in the Equipped plan, and individual coaching, included in the Renewed plan, add live, scheduled sessions with a consultant on top of that community access.

Do I need a paid plan to join the community?

Yes, an active Supported, Equipped, or Renewed membership is required. Community access is included the moment you sign up, at any tier.

Ready to Connect With Other Parents?

Community membership comes with every Redeemed Arrow plan. Join today and open the community anytime from our home page.
