By accessing or using the Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Policy. We reserve the right to update or modify this Policy at any time. Material changes will be indicated by a revised Effective Date posted at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically. Your continued use of the Website after any modification constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.

2. User Acknowledgment and Consent

By accessing or using the Website, you acknowledge and consent to the following:

  • (a) We collect the categories of personal information described in Section 3 of this Policy.
  • (b) We use your personal information for the purposes described in Section 4 of this Policy.
  • (c) We may share your personal information with the categories of third parties described in Section 5 of this Policy.
  • (d) We use cookies and other tracking technologies as described in Section 6 of this Policy, and you consent to such use unless you exercise the opt-out rights described in Section 9.

If you do not consent to the data practices described in this Policy, you must discontinue use of the Website. Certain features of the Website may not function properly if you opt out of particular data collection practices as described in Section 9.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide To Us Directly

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you interact with the Website, including when you submit an inquiry through the Contact Us form, register for or purchase services, or otherwise communicate with us. The categories of personal information we may collect directly from you include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Postal address
  • Telephone number
  • Customer number, order number, or other unique identifier
  • Payment card information (collected and processed through a third-party payment processor)

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, certain information is collected automatically through cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and similar tracking technologies. This automatically collected information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed and time spent on the Website
  • Referring URL
  • Website analytics data collected through Google Analytics or similar services
  • Advertising tracking data collected through cookies, Facebook pixel, and similar tools

For more information about how we use cookies and tracking technologies, and how you may opt out, see Section 6 (Cookies and Tracking Technologies) and Section 9 (Your Choices and Opt-Out Rights).

3.3 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, government-issued identification numbers, financial account credentials, biometric data, precise geolocation data, or health or medical information through the Website. Please do not submit this type of information through the Contact Us form or any other feature of the Website. See Section 7 for important information regarding health information and HIPAA.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • (a) to respond to your inquiries submitted through the Contact Us form or other Website features;
  • (b) to provide, administer, and improve our coaching services and Website;
  • (c) to process transactions and send related information, including confirmations and receipts;
  • (d) to send you administrative communications, such as updates to this Policy or our Terms and Conditions;
  • (e) to send you marketing or promotional communications about our services, where you have not opted out of receiving such communications;
  • (f) to analyze Website usage and improve user experience through analytics tools including Google Analytics;
  • (g) to deliver targeted advertising and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns through tools such as the Facebook pixel;
  • (h) to enforce our Website Terms and Conditions and protect the rights, property, and safety of the Company, our users, and others;
  • (i) to comply with applicable legal obligations; and
  • (j) to carry out any other lawful purpose.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects without human review.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, pixel tags, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your use of the Website and to deliver advertising. The following categories of tracking technologies are used on the Website:

  • (a) Essential cookies: necessary for the Website to function and cannot be disabled.
  • (b) Analytics cookies: used to understand how visitors interact with the Website, including through Google Analytics. Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, time on site, and referral source. Information generated by Google Analytics cookies is transmitted to and stored by Google. For more information, see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com.
  • (c) Advertising and retargeting cookies: used to deliver advertisements relevant to your interests and to measure advertising effectiveness, including through the Facebook pixel. Facebook pixel transmits certain Website usage data to Meta Platforms, Inc. For more information, see Meta's privacy policy at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through browser settings. You may also opt out of specific tracking technologies as described in Section 9. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Website.

9. Your Choices and Opt-Out Rights

You have the following choices regarding our collection and use of your personal information:

9.1 Marketing Communications

If you no longer wish to receive marketing or promotional emails from us, you may opt out by clicking the “unsubscribe” link included in any marketing email we send you, or by contacting us at info@redeemedarrow.com with the subject line "Unsubscribe." You may opt out of Google Analytics data collection by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. You may also adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, which will limit Google Analytics tracking.

9.3 Facebook Pixel and Meta Advertising

You may opt out of interest-based advertising delivered through the Facebook pixel by adjusting your ad preferences in your Facebook account settings, or by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page at optout.aboutads.info. Most web browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through browser settings. The method for doing so varies by browser; refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Website and may prevent certain features from working properly.

9.5 Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” (“DNT”) feature that sends a signal to websites requesting that your browsing activity not be tracked. The Website complies with applicable law with regard to the alteration of its data collection practices in response to DNT signals.

If you are a California resident, you may have certain rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (“CCPA”), if applicable to our business, including:

  • (a) Right to Know: You may request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources from which it was collected, the purposes for collection and use, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • (b) Right to Delete: You may request deletion of personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • (c) Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • (d) Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing: We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under the CCPA without providing a mechanism to opt out. If we do sell or share personal information, we will update this Policy and provide an opt-out mechanism as required by law.
  • (e) Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: To the extent we collect sensitive personal information as defined under the CCPA and the law applies to our activities, you may have the right to limit our use of that information to purposes permitted by law.
  • (f) Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To submit a CCPA rights request, contact us at info@redeemedarrow.com with the subject line “Data Request – California Resident.” We will respond to verifiable requests within the timeframe required by the CCPA as applicable to our business. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

9.7 Illinois Residents

If you are an Illinois resident, you may have rights under the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act (815 ILCS 530) with respect to the personal information you have shared with us. To submit a request to access, correct, or delete your personal information, contact us at info@redeemedarrow.com with the subject line “Data Request – Illinois Resident.” We will respond in accordance with applicable Illinois law. You will be notified in the event of any unauthorized disclosure of your information consistent with Illinois law.
