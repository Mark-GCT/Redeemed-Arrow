The Vault: Video Training for Foster & Adoptive Parents

On-demand behavioral training is a growing library of short video lessons, called The Vault, that walk you through trauma-informed care, basic ABA principles, and everyday behavioral education at your own pace.

Every video runs about 15 minutes, short enough to fit into nap time, a lunch break, or a few quiet minutes after bedtime. Watch what's relevant right now, rewatch it before a hard week, and work through the full library whenever you're ready.

Common Questions

On-Demand Behavioral Training FAQ

What is The Vault?

The Vault is Redeemed Arrow's on-demand video library. Each video runs about 15 minutes and walks you through a specific trauma-informed care, ABA, or behavioral education topic you can apply right away.

Who is on-demand behavioral training for?

It's designed for foster, adoptive, and kinship parents who want to learn trauma-informed strategies and basic behavioral principles at their own pace, without waiting for a scheduled call.

How long are the training videos?

Most Vault videos run about 15 minutes, short enough to watch during a nap, a lunch break, or a few quiet minutes after bedtime.

What topics does The Vault cover?

The Vault covers trauma-informed care, basic Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) principles, and everyday behavioral education topics foster and adoptive families deal with.

Is The Vault included in every membership plan?

Yes. Full Vault access is included starting with the Supported plan and carries through the Equipped and Renewed plans, with early access to new videos on Equipped and Renewed. See membership plans.

How do I access The Vault?

Once you join any Redeemed Arrow plan, The Vault is available in your member area. Watch any video anytime and revisit it as often as you'd like.

Ready to Start Learning at Your Own Pace?

The Vault comes with every Redeemed Arrow plan. Join today and start working through trauma-informed, ABA-based video training whenever it fits your day.
