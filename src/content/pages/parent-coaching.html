Individual Parent Coaching

Private, One-on-One Coaching for Foster & Adoptive Parents

Individual parent coaching is private, one-on-one guidance from a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) or senior consultant, built entirely around your child's history and your family's goals.

Every foster and adoptive family carries a different story. Individual coaching gives you a dedicated space, separate from group calls and community threads, to work through the behaviors, routines, and relationship repair that are unique to your home.

Common Questions

Individual Parent Coaching FAQ

What is individual parent coaching?

Individual parent coaching is a private, one-on-one session with a senior behavior consultant who helps you apply trauma-informed, ABA-based strategies to your child's specific behaviors, in your own home, at your own pace.

Who is individual parent coaching for?

It's designed for foster, adoptive, and kinship parents who want dedicated, personalized support beyond group calls, especially families navigating trauma, attachment challenges, or behaviors that need focused, one-on-one attention.

How do I get access to individual coaching sessions?

Individual coaching is included in the Renewed membership plan. Once you join the membership, your monthly one-on-one session is scheduled directly with your consultant.

How is individual coaching different from group coaching?

Group coaching sessions are shared calls where members work through common topics together. Individual coaching is a private session focused entirely on your family, your child, and your specific goals.

What can we talk about during a coaching session?

Anything tied to your child's behavior and your family's dynamics, including de-escalation strategies, daily routines, trauma responses, attachment repair, school or caseworker challenges, and progress on your personalized behavior plan.

How often are individual coaching sessions?

Renewed members receive one private coaching session per month, along with priority 24-hour response time for direct-message support between sessions.
