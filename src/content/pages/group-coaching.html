Is Group Coaching Right for Your Family?

Group coaching gives you a recurring, real-time space to learn alongside other families. Here's exactly who it's for, what happens on a call, and why parents keep coming back.

  • Who It's For

    Foster, adoptive, and kinship parents who want real-time coaching and peer support, especially families who learn well by hearing how others handle similar trauma and behavior challenges.

  • What It Includes

    Two live video sessions each month led by a senior consultant, an ongoing Ask-Me-Anything thread for questions between calls, and on-demand replays if you can't make it live.

    You're not the only parent working through this. Group coaching normalizes the struggle, multiplies the strategies available to you, and keeps you accountable to consistent, trauma-informed routines.

Common Questions

Group Coaching FAQ

What is group coaching?

Group coaching is a live, video-based session where foster, adoptive, and kinship parents meet twice a month with a trauma-informed consultant to talk through real parenting challenges together.

Who is group coaching for?

It's designed for parents who want consistent, real-time coaching and peer support, especially those who learn well by hearing how other families handle similar trauma and behavior challenges.

How do I join a group coaching session?

Group coaching is included in the Equipped membership plan. Once you join the membership, you'll get the schedule and login link for every live session.

How is group coaching different from individual coaching?

Group coaching sessions are shared calls where several members work through common topics together. Individual coaching, included in the Renewed plan, is a private session focused entirely on your family.

What if I can't make a live session?

Every group coaching session is recorded. If you miss a live call, you can watch the on-demand replay and still post follow-up questions in the Ask-Me-Anything thread.

How often are group coaching sessions?

Equipped members get two live group coaching sessions every month, plus ongoing access to the Ask-Me-Anything thread between calls.
