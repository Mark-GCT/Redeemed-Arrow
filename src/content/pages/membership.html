Common Questions

Membership FAQ

What membership plans does Redeemed Arrow offer?

Redeemed Arrow offers three membership tiers: Supported ($47/month), Equipped ($97/month), and Renewed ($147/month). Each tier builds on the one before it, adding live coaching and more personalized support.

What's included in every plan?

Every plan, starting with Supported, includes full access to The Vault training library, the resource library, community support on Circle, on-demand replays of live sessions, and a new member cohort training session.

What does the Equipped plan add?

Equipped includes everything in Supported, plus live group coaching sessions twice a month, access to an Ask-Me-Anything thread, and early access to new content and resources.

What does the Renewed plan add?

Renewed includes everything in Equipped, plus one private 1:1 coaching session per month with a senior consultant, a personalized behavior plan, and priority 24-hour response time for direct-message support.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade to a higher tier as your family's needs change. Contact our team for help moving between plans.

How do I sign up?

Choose the plan that fits your family above to join through our community platform, or contact us first if you have questions about which tier is right for you.
