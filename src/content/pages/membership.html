Common Questions

Membership FAQ

What membership plans does Redeemed Arrow offer?

Redeemed Arrow offers three membership tiers: Supported ($47/month), Equipped ($97/month), and Renewed ($147/month). Each tier builds on the one before it, adding live coaching and more personalized support.

What's included in every plan?

Every plan, starting with Supported, includes full access to The Vault training library, the resource library, community support on Circle, on-demand replays of live sessions, and a new member cohort training session.

What does the Equipped plan add?

Equipped includes everything in Supported, plus live group coaching sessions twice a month, access to an Ask-Me-Anything thread, and early access to new content and resources.

What does the Renewed plan add?

Renewed includes everything in Equipped, plus one private 1:1 coaching session per month with a senior consultant, a personalized behavior plan, and priority 24-hour response time for direct-message support.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can change your monthly membership tier at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. Contact our team for help moving between plans.

How do I sign up?

Choose the plan that fits your family above to join through our community platform, or contact us first if you have questions about which tier is right for you.

Is this therapy or clinical services?

No. Redeemed Arrow is a consulting and education community — not a clinical mental health service and not a substitute for therapy. We provide behavior strategies, parent training, and support rooted in ABA and trauma-informed frameworks. If your child needs clinical services, we encourage you to pursue those alongside membership here.

What if I'm not a licensed foster parent yet?

You are still welcome here. Many members are in the process of getting licensed or are considering fostering. The training vault and community are valuable at any stage of the journey.

What does the free week include?

Your free week gives you full access to the tier you select — including the training vault, community, and resources. No credit card is required to start. At the end of 7 days you can continue by entering your payment details or cancel with no charge.

Is the community faith-based?

Redeemed Arrow is a faith-founded practice. Consistent with our clinical and ethical standards, our services are delivered using trauma-informed, evidence-based methods, applied equally to every client regardless of religious background.

Does my agency qualify for a group license?

Yes. We offer group licensing for foster care agencies who want to provide this as a resource for their families. Contact us directly to discuss pricing and setup for your organization.
